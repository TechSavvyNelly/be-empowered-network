Terms of use

What this website is, what it is not, and what you can expect from us. Written to be read, not to be skipped.

Last updated 3 November 2026.

This is not an emergency service

Nothing here is monitored around the clock. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 112 in Nigeria, or go to the nearest hospital. Do not wait for a reply from us.

We are also not a clinical service. We do not diagnose, we do not prescribe, and nothing on this site is medical advice or a substitute for seeing a qualified professional.

Who we are

This site is run by Be Empowered Network, Incorporated Trustees, registration CAC/IT/NO 140796, a non-profit registered in Nigeria. Contact: hello@beempowerednetwork.org.

By using the site you accept these terms. If you do not, please stop using it.

What we offer

Free peer support circles, school and workplace programmes, a directory of mental health services in Nigeria, and general information about mental health. Peer support means trained volunteers with lived experience, sitting alongside you. It is not therapy and it is not a replacement for it.

We do our best to keep the site available, but we cannot promise it will never be down or interrupted.

The directory

The directory lists hospitals, helplines, NGOs and online services run by other people. We compile it in good faith and check it periodically, but:

  • We do not endorse, vet, supervise or take responsibility for any listed service.
  • Details change. Always confirm fees, opening hours and availability directly with the service.
  • Check a practitioner’s registration with the relevant Nigerian professional body before you pay anyone.
  • If a listing is wrong, out of date or has treated you badly, tell us and we will look into it.

Bee, the chat companion

Bee is an automated self-help guide. It is not a person, not a counsellor and not an emergency service. Part of what you type may be processed by an AI provider outside Nigeria — our privacy notice explains exactly when. Treat anything it says as general information, never as advice about your particular situation.

Your account

  • Accounts are for people aged 18 or over.
  • Give accurate details and keep your password to yourself. Tell us if you think someone else has got into your account.
  • You can close your account whenever you like by emailing us. We will not make it difficult.
  • We may suspend an account that is being used to harass people, break these terms or endanger others. Where we reasonably can, we will tell you why first.

Using the site decently

Please do not use this site to harass, threaten or impersonate anyone, to send spam, to scrape or overload it, to break into any part of it, or to post anything unlawful. Our forms and circles exist for people who need them.

Donations

Donations go to Be Empowered Network’s own account, held in the organisation’s name. We will send a receipt if you ask. Donations are generally not refundable once given, but if you have made a mistake, contact us and we will do what we reasonably can. We will never pressure you to give, and we will not pass your details to another fundraiser.

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. The site works with a keyboard alone, respects reduced-motion settings, keeps text readable at 200% zoom, and works with JavaScript turned off for everything that matters. We know we will have missed things. If any part of this site is hard for you to use, email us and we will fix it — that is a bug, not a request.
