    Looking back

    Highlights from past events

    Some of the moments that have shaped the network since 2017.

    October 2025

    World Mental Health Day: a community conversation on work

    Volunteers, an employer and a clinician sat on one panel to talk honestly about burnout, and what a manager can actually do about it. The afternoon closed with a peer circle taster, and several attendees went on to join our monthly online circle.

    The strongest moment came from the floor: a young professional describing the first time a colleague simply asked "are you okay, really?" and waited for the answer.

    June 2025

    New facilitator cohort trained

    A full day of training on active listening, group safety and referral pathways for our newest circle facilitators, several of whom first came to the network as participants.

    March 2025

    Mental Health Week in secondary schools

    A week of student assemblies and teacher sessions with partner schools, covering stress, anxiety and where to get help, so a young person's first disclosure is met with understanding.

    October 2024

    Walk & Talk with open-mic stories

    Our annual World Mental Health Day walk, followed by open-mic stories from people with lived experience. The format has become a fixture of our calendar.
