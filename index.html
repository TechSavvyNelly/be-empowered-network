Making it normal to talk about mental health in Nigeria.

Be Empowered Network runs peer support circles, school and workplace programmes and community campaigns, so that no one has to face anxiety, depression or grief in silence.

What we do

Three ways we show up for people.

Everything we run is built around one idea: recovery is easier when you're not doing it alone.

01

Peer support circles

Small, facilitated groups where people meet others going through the same thing: anxiety, depression, bereavement, burnout. No diagnosis needed, no cost, no judgement. Circles meet in person and online.

How circles work
02

Education & outreach

Mental health literacy sessions for secondary schools, universities, faith communities and workplaces, teaching people to spot the signs early and know what to do next.

03

Advocacy & awareness

Campaigns that challenge stigma, from World Mental Health Day activations to conversations with employers and community leaders about care that is affordable and accessible.

Our story

Started in 2017 by someone who had been there.

Be Empowered Network was founded in February 2017 by Nnenna Kalu, a tech and health professional, out of her own experience of depression and the realisation that the women around her were carrying pressure and insecurity with nowhere safe to put it down.

She set out to build what she could not find: a safe space for honest interaction and wholeness, with professional help alongside it, and to teach communities to respond with care instead of shame.

In December 2019 we were registered by the Corporate Affairs Commission as an Incorporated Trustee (CAC/IT/NO 140796), governed by a board of trustees. Today we are a volunteer-led network reaching people through peer support, education and public campaigns.

Need support?

Getting help is simpler than it feels.

You don't need a diagnosis, a referral or the right words. Start wherever you are.

  1. If it's urgent

    If you or someone near you is in danger right now, call 112. Then come back to us when you're safe. We'll still be here.

  2. Talk to someone

    Email or message us. A trained volunteer will reply, listen, and help you work out what kind of support fits.

  3. Join a circle

    We'll match you to a peer support circle: in person where we run them, or online from anywhere in Nigeria.

  4. Go further if you need to

    When professional care is the right next step, we'll help you find affordable therapists and clinics and stay alongside you.
